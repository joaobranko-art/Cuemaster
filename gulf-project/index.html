Who We Are

Your Bridge Between Portugal & the Gulf

JB Investment Group, led by João Branco, serves as the dedicated Portuguese liaison to the Middle East — identifying, curating and presenting high-value investment ventures to Gulf-based investors seeking partnership with established Portuguese enterprises.
